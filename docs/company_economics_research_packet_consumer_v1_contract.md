## Product value and scope

The released economics matrix exposes nine observation/interpretation dimensions,
source citations and temporal exclusions but is an opt-in library without a Human/AI
packet consumer. Already-qualified PNJ/PVD/FPT facts are retained and portable;
users should not have to manually flatten current H1, historical annual, USD/VND,
reported earnings components and missing structural proof into an AI prompt.
Extend long_term_company_economics_evidence/v1 and the existing Human/AI packet,
not a financial/decision engine. Keep the matrix's OFFLINE_OPT_IN_ONLY contract.

Add a verified, non-voting economics packet view and a descriptive comparison over
selected matrices. Preserve every exact value, period, currency, scope, assurance,
source reference, knowledge/retrieval/publication time and restriction. Keep
observation versus current-observation versus interpretation states separate.
Comparison groups only identical explicit covered dates/metric/scope/currency/
statement/context/component basis. Missing dates do not become a compatible label.
Groups show retained observations; no arithmetic, winner, rank, normalized economics,
EPS, annualization, FX, TTM, sizing or price/return estimate. Unknown interpretation
and independent structural flags never become a company quality or action verdict.

The existing Human/AI builder accepts an optional sealed matrix; invalid identity,
subject, timestamps, shape or directional/normalization boundary fails closed.
Existing callers without this input keep byte-equivalent output. The optional
canonical packet reference stays a reference; the economics cutoff is consultation
knowledge time, not proof those facts existed in the sealed decision. Add a read-only
research economics CLI using explicit qualified JSONL and cutoff, selected tickers
and optional verified canonical packet. stdout only; no Daily registration.
All roots and temp used by this scope are on C:, no W: dependency.

Offline acceptance uses the existing tracked qualified overlay, adversarial cutoff,
conflict/hash/shape/subject cases, USD and annual-context restrictions, compatible
and incompatible comparison groups, preserved no-economics packets, read-only CLI
and unchanged numerical/operational consumers. All four hosted CI jobs required.
Future live acceptance is not claimed. Fiscal calendar needs its exact source proof;
structural economics, recurrence/normalization, strict shares/valuation and genuine
outcome maturity remain blocked by their existing evidence gates.

This exposes existing source-bound analytical depth and safeguards its use, with

## Offline owner use

`python stocklookup.py research economics --facts derived/financial-evidence-currency-refresh-v1/qualified_official_facts.jsonl --cutoff 2026-10-08T15:00:00+07:00 --ticker PNJ --ticker PVD --ticker FPT`

Repeat `--ticker` for a selected study group (maximum 32, duplicates rejected).
An optional `--canonical-packet` names an existing sealed packet containing all
selected subjects; its complete current validator and both identity fields must
pass. Its original session is retained independently and cannot be later than the
consultation cutoff date. Nothing enriches that file
or backfills its decision-time evidence. Dates on this inspection are consultation
cutoffs, not claims of a new completed trading session.

The stock section embeds the complete sealed matrix under a research view wrapper;
the original matrix digest remains verifiable. Uncertainty shows independent flags
and normalization limits; counter-thesis shows unknown interpretation and blockers.
These mixed fact/unknown fields are DATA_WARNING, not a new company-quality FACT.
Reserved fields cannot replace caller fields. A content digest verifies integrity,
not authenticity/PIT: qualification remains the existing upstream source contract's
responsibility. Re-sealing invented facts does not authenticate them.

Comparison uses explicit covered dates, metric, currency, scope, period type,
statement family, duration/instant nature and component semantics. Each observation
also retains its reporting label, temporal fitness, assurance and citation. A matching
reported basis is explicitly not economic equivalence or peer-method qualification.
Rows without explicit valid covered dates remain visible and unpaired. No arithmetic
is performed, including subtraction, ratios, add-backs or ordering by amount.

JSONL syntax, duplicate keys and nonfinite numbers fail closed before any success;
existing producer semantic/time exclusions stay visible, including later-retrieved
evidence at an earlier cutoff. Hashes before/after prevent a changed source file from
publishing an AVAILABLE result. Output is stdout only; UNAVAILABLE exits 2.

## Financial-quality decision bridge (offline, non-voting)

Owner scope `CURRENT_RESEARCH_FINANCIAL_QUALITY_DECISION_BRIDGE_V1`, based on
merged PR192 main `fe3e6e5ed87136d5d75e923f1be69a14311066ea`.
Extend the existing `interim_earnings_cash_research` consumer and this read-only

`python stocklookup.py research financial-quality --fundamental <existing-projection.json>
--cutoff <timezone-bearing-consultation-time> --ticker PNJ --format markdown`

The input is an existing `market_wide_current_fundamental_research/v1` projection,
with both content identity fields verified. Its source session and cutoff remain
independent of consultation time. Field knowledge, retrieval and known publication
must be eligible at the original source cutoff; consultation cannot backfill facts
into a sealed session. Source files are hashed before and after, stdout only.
A valid content hash is integrity, not authenticity, factual correctness or a grant
of Producer authority. This interface consumes the retained admitted projection;
it does not ingest a Hub report or qualify raw cells by trusting caller labels.
Source admission, assurance, source identity, D-1, units, entity and conflict gates
remain upstream requirements; field consumer gates are rechecked.

`financial_quality_decision_bridge` is a separate opt-in function in the existing
earnings/cash module. Calls to the released interim context are byte-preserved.
It retains exact reported profit, source-labelled disposal/provision components,
cash and balance observations. Group cash is paired only with group total PAT;
same document, dates, scope, currency, period type, assurance and comparative basis
are required. Existing deterministic CFO/PAT arithmetic is descriptive, only over
positive PAT. No parent-profit/group-cash ratio, component/PAT adjustment, normalized
EPS, tax estimate, annualization, TTM or currency conversion is introduced.
The existing selected economics comparison still performs no arithmetic.

The investor answer separates confirming observations, accounting counterevidence,
unsupported conclusions, evidence needed and source-testable thesis invalidation.
Reported disposal is not automatically non-recurring; a provision reconciliation
is neither cash received nor an earnings add-back. A profit/cash gap does not prove
receivable causality or overdue status. Missing working-capital notes, contractual
obligations, OCI/tax/attribution and comparable fiscal evidence remain named gaps.
There is currently no admitted OCI or working-capital bridge in this input contract;
unsupported numbers are not copied from Hub. Banks/securities/unresolved entities
receive no industrial CFO/PAT or net-debt interpretation. Existing upstream research
metrics/trajectory remain labelled context, without a whole-company WAIT/verdict.

Valuation explanation identifies why reported profit alone cannot qualify a recurring
earnings denominator or establish an attractive multiple. No multiple, intrinsic
value, target, probability, causal profitability claim or capital action is computed.
The Human/AI stock, uncertainty and counter-thesis sections use `DATA_WARNING`;
capital decision remains human-owned. Source limits survive alongside useful facts.
